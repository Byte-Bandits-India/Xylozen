## 1. Brand Identity & Positioning

### 1.1 Brand Foundation
Xylozen Technologies positions itself as an international-standard engineering partner. Operating from Chennai, India and serving clients worldwide, the visual identity and editorial voice must convey **rigor, precision, engineering judgment, and technical maturity**.

### 1.2 The Tagline
- **Official Phrasing:** `Technology . Innovation . Solutions`
- **Formatting Rules:**
  - Sentence case with spaced periods (` . `).
  - Always rendered in **Inter Tight Light (Weight 300)** with loose tracking (`0.14em`).
  - Must sit on its own line directly beneath the logo.
  - **Color:** Silver-light (`#E7EAEE`) on dark surfaces; Muted ink (`#6B7480`) on light surfaces.
  - **Restrictions:** Never used as an H1/H2 headline, never embedded inline within a running paragraph.

## 2. Logo & Mark Architecture

### 2.1 Construction
The primary logo consists of two core components:
1. **The 'X' Symbol:** An angular glyph engineered from two converging strokes:
   - One stroke rendered with the brand signature gradient (`#265DC3` to `#051C50`).
   - One stroke rendered in brushed metallic finish.
2. **The Wordmark:** The name `xylozen` in lowercase Inter Tight typography.

> **Key Rule:** The 'X' symbol can be used independently as a standalone favicon, app icon, or avatar. The lowercase wordmark **must never** be used alone without the 'X'.

### 2.2 Variants & Usage Matrix
| Context | Background Plate | Symbol 'X' Treatment | Wordmark Treatment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary (Dark)** | Navy (`#051C50`) or Pure Black (`#000000`) | Gradient (`#4C8BEF` → `#265DC3`) | Metallic Silver (`#E7EAEE` gradient) |
| **Alternate (Light)** | Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Light Surface (`#F3F3F3`) | Gradient (`#265DC3` → `#051C50`) | Solid Navy (`#051C50`) |
| **Monochrome / Print** | Single-ink print, embroidery, stamping | Solid `#051C50` (on light) | Solid `#051C50` (on light) |
| **Monochrome (Inverted)** | Single-ink on dark substrates | Solid `#FFFFFF` | Solid `#FFFFFF` |

### 2.3 Clear Space & Minimum Sizing
- **Clear Space Perimeter:** A minimum boundary equal to **half the height of the X (`½X`)** must be maintained on all 4 sides. No typography, grid lines, imagery borders, or UI components may intrude into this zone.
- **Minimum Digital Size:**
  - Full Lockup: `120px` width.
  - Standalone 'X' Mark: `24px` width/height.
- **Minimum Print Size:**
  - Full Lockup: `30mm` width.
  - Standalone 'X' Mark: `8mm` width/height.

### 2.4 Logo Do's & Don'ts
- ✅ **DO** mount the logo exclusively on solid navy, black, or white backgrounds.
- ✅ **DO** utilize the standalone 'X' mark in compact viewports when brand presence is already established.
- ❌ **DON'T** place the metallic silver wordmark on white or pale photographic backgrounds (lacks contrast).
- ❌ **DON'T** recolor, outline, tilt, rotate, condense, stretch, or apply drop shadows to the logo.
- ❌ **DON'T** overlay the logo directly onto busy, high-frequency imagery without a solid backing plate.
- ❌ **DON'T** recreate the wordmark using live font text — always embed official vector assets.

## 4. Color Usage Ratios & Accessibility Compliance

### 4.1 The 60-30-10 Rule
To maintain an executive, institutional appearance rather than looking like an unfocused template, page real estate must adhere to:
- **60% Neutrals:** White (`#FFFFFF`), Light Grey Surface (`#F3F3F3`), Line (`#E2E6ED`).
- **30% Blue Family:** Primary Blue (`#265DC3`), Navy (`#051C50`), Mid Blue (`#163D8A`).
- **10% Accents & Actions:** Shared strictly across Teal, Gold, Violet, and Action Orange.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────┬───────────┐
│                    60% Neutrals                        │      30% Blue Family     │ 10% Accent│
│            (#FFFFFF, #F3F3F3, #E2E6ED)                 │ (#265DC3, #163D8A, #051C)│ (CTA/Tags)│
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────┴───────────┘
```

### 4.2 WCAG Contrast Matrix on White (`#FFFFFF`)
| Color | Hex | Contrast Ratio | Permitted Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Deep** | `#051C50` | **16.3 : 1** | Any typography size, any weight (AAA) |
| **Primary Blue** | `#265DC3` | **6.1 : 1** | Body copy, links, badges, UI labels (AA+) |
| **Violet** | `#7A5AF8` | **4.5 : 1** | Text at `16px` and larger |
| **Action Deep** | `#D93A16` | **4.0 : 1** | Text at `18px` semibold and larger, or small button labels |
| **Action Orange** | `#FF5A2C` | **3.1 : 1** | Fills and large text only (White button text must be `≥16px` semibold) |
| **Teal UI** | `#00A3AD` | **3.1 : 1** | Graphical fills, icons, chart lines |
| **Teal** | `#00C2CB` | **2.2 : 1** | Dark backgrounds only (forbidden as text on white) |
| **Gold** | `#F5A524` | **2.0 : 1** | Graphical fills only (forbidden as text on white) |

### 4.3 Approved vs. Forbidden Pairings
- **Approved Dark Layout:** Navy (`#051C50`) background + Silver-Light (`#C6D3EC`) body copy + Teal (`#00C2CB`) icons/links + Orange (`#FF5A2C`) CTA button.
- **Approved Light Layout:** White/Surface background + Navy (`#051C50`) headings + Ink (`#1F2733`) body copy + Primary Blue (`#265DC3`) links + Tinted service badges.
- 🚫 **Forbidden Pairings:**
  - Never place Teal (`#00C2CB`) text on Primary Blue (`#265DC3`).
  - Never place Violet (`#7A5AF8`) text on Navy (`#051C50`).
  - Never set pure white (`#FFFFFF`) text for long-form body copy on dark panels (use `#C6D3EC` to prevent eye strain).

## 5. Typography System

### 5.1 Typefaces
- **Primary Typeface:** **Inter Tight**
  - Fallbacks: `ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`
  - Purpose: Universal typography for headlines, body copy, navigation, and buttons.
- **Monospace Typeface:** **JetBrains Mono**
  - Fallbacks: `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Monaco, Consolas, monospace`
  - Purpose: Code blocks, metrics, technical data, step indicators (`01`, `02`), and contrast calculations.

### 5.2 Type Scale
| Role | Size | Line Height | Weight | Tracking | Case | Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Display** | `56px` (`3.5rem`) | `1.05` | 600 (SemiBold) | `-0.02em` | Sentence case | Hero primary title |
| **Heading 1** | `40px` (`2.5rem`) | `1.10` | 600 (SemiBold) | `-0.02em` | Sentence case | Page main titles |
| **Heading 2** | `30px` (`1.875rem`) | `1.20` | 600 (SemiBold) | `-0.015em` | Sentence case | Section headers |
| **Heading 3** | `22px` (`1.375rem`) | `1.35` | 600 (SemiBold) | `-0.01em` | Sentence case | Card and sub-section titles |
| **Lead Paragraph** | `19px` (`1.1875rem`)| `1.60` | 400 (Regular) | `0` | Sentence case | Section introductory blurbs |
| **Body Text** | `16px` (`1.0rem`) | `1.70` | 400 (Regular) | `0` | Sentence case | Standard descriptive paragraphs |
| **Small Text** | `14px` (`0.875rem`)| `1.60` | 400 (Regular) | `0` | Sentence case | Notes, form labels, secondary meta |
| **Caption / Mono** | `13px` (`0.8125rem`)| `1.50` | 500 (Medium) | `0.04em` | Sentence / Title | Eyebrows, step indicators, table heads |
| **Brand Tagline** | `17px` (`1.0625rem`)| `1.40` | 300 (Light) | `0.14em` | Title case | Sub-logo lockup |

### 5.3 Editorial Typography Rules
1. **Reading Width:** Paragraph width must be constrained to a maximum of **68 characters (`max-width: 68ch`)** for readability.
2. **Heading Casing:** Headlines are strictly **Sentence case**. No ALL-CAPS headlines.
3. **No Multicolor Words:** Do not color a single word differently within a heading for emphasis (e.g., avoid "Precision **Operationalized**" in two different hues).
4. **Dark Mode Text:** On dark backgrounds, headings use Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), while body text uses Muted Slate Light (`#C6D3EC`).

## 6. UI Component Specifications

### 6.1 Buttons
Buttons establish a clear 3-tier hierarchy:
```
[ Action CTA: Orange ] > [ Primary: Blue ] > [ Ghost: White with Line Border ]
```

| Button Tier | Background | Text Color | Border | Hover State | Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary CTA** | `#FF5A2C` | `#FFFFFF` | None | Background `#D93A16` | **Max 1 per viewport.** Reserved for highest-value conversion. |
| **Primary Brand** | `#265DC3` | `#FFFFFF` | None | Background `#163D8A` | Everyday action (Explore services, submit, view case studies). |
| **Ghost / Tertiary**| `#FFFFFF` | `#051C50` | `1px solid #E2E6ED` | Background `#F3F3F3` | Supporting actions, documentation, cancel. |

- **Button Dimensions:**
  - Border Radius: `8px` (`rounded-lg`)
  - Padding: `12px` vertical, `22px` horizontal
  - Font: `15px` to `16px`, Inter Tight, Weight 600
  - Focus Ring: `2px solid #265DC3` with `3px` offset

### 6.2 Service Badges & Categorical Tags
Service lines maintain a dedicated color hue across the website, presentations, and client deliverables:

| Service Line | Background Tint (~12%) | Text Color (Cleared 4.5:1) | Hex Pair |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Web & Software** | Soft Blue Tint | Brand Mid Blue | `bg: #E4ECFB`, `text: #163D8A` |
| **Agentic AI & Automation** | Soft Violet Tint | Deep Violet | `bg: #EDE9FE`, `text: #4B32B0` |
| **Ecommerce** | Soft Teal Tint | Deep Teal UI | `bg: #D8F5F7`, `text: #00676E` |
| **Consulting / Strategy** | Soft Gold Tint | Deep Gold Ochre | `bg: #FDF0D9`, `text: #8A5A05` |

- **Badge Dimensions:**
  - Border Radius: `6px`
  - Padding: `4px` vertical, `11px` horizontal
  - Font: `12.5px`, Inter Tight, Weight 500

### 6.3 Surfaces & Content Cards
- **Card Styling:** Solid White (`#FFFFFF`) background with a crisp `1px solid #E2E6ED` border and `14px` border radius (`rounded-[14px]`).
- **No Drop Shadows:** **Strict rule:** Cards do not use drop shadows (`box-shadow: none`). Separation is created purely through hairline borders and background tone shifts.
- **Section Banding:** Alternates between Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) and Neutral Surface (`#F3F3F3`).
- **Dark Section Bands:** Rendered in Flat Navy (`#051C50`) or Signature Gradient (`135deg, #265DC3 -> #163D8A -> #051C50`).
- **Adjacency Rule:** Two gradient sections must never touch consecutively.

## 7. Voice, Tone & Copywriting Standards

Xylozen sells engineering judgment to international stakeholders who may never visit the office in person. Every sentence must establish technical competence.

### 7.1 The Three Writing Rules
1. **Plain verbs, specific nouns:** Eliminate adjectives that impersonate evidence.
2. **Quantifiable outcomes over empty claims:** State what was built, for whom, and what changed.
3. **No enterprise cliches:** Ban words like *synergy, cutting-edge, empower, revolutionize, robust, seamless*.

### 7.2 Writing Contrast
| ❌ What NOT to Write | ✅ What Xylozen Writes |
| :--- | :--- |
| *"We leverage cutting-edge solutions to empower your business transformation."* | *"Cut checkout drop-off by 22% for a UK apparel retailer."* |
| *"Our world-class AI agents automate your workflows seamlessly."* | *"A digital operations team that executes 12 hours of inventory sync weekly."* |
| *"Delivering robust enterprise platforms built to scale."* | *"Software that connects the factory floor directly to the ERP dashboard."* |
