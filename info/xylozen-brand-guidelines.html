01Logo

The mark is an angular X built from two strokes — one carrying the brand gradient, one in brushed metal — followed by the lowercase wordmark. The X may be used alone; the wordmark may not.

Versions

Xylozen

Primary, on navy or black. Wordmark in metallic silver, X in #265DC3.

Xylozen

On white. Wordmark switches to solid navy #051C50 — the metallic gradient loses all contrast on light backgrounds.

A one-colour version exists for stamping, embroidery and single-ink print: the entire lockup in #051C50, or in white on dark.

Clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space on all four sides equal to half the height of the X. Nothing enters that field — no text, no rule, no image edge. Minimum sizes: 120px wide on screen and 30mm in print for the full lockup; 24px and 8mm for the X used alone as an app icon or favicon.

Do and don't

DoPlace the logo on a solid navy, black or white plate.
DoUse the X alone where the name already appears nearby.
Don'tPut the metallic wordmark on white or on a light photo.
Don'tRecolour, outline, rotate, stretch or add a drop shadow.
Don'tSet the lockup over busy imagery without a plate behind it.
Don'tRebuild the wordmark by typing it — always place the supplied file.

03Colour in use

Balance

60% neutral
30% blue family
10%

Roughly sixty parts white and grey, thirty parts blue and navy, ten parts everything else. Teal, gold, violet and orange share that last tenth between them — past it the brand stops reading as premium and starts reading as a template.

White text on the orange CTA reaches 3.1:1, so button labels sit at 16px semibold or larger. For a small orange link or a compact button, use #D93A16 instead.

Never place teal on blue, or violet on navy, as text — the hues are too close in value. Both are fine as fills separated by a rule or by whitespace.

04Typography

Inter Tight throughout, in three weights: 300 for the tagline, 400 for body, 600 for headings. Nothing else. JetBrains Mono appears only for code and technical values.

Body measure stays under 68 characters. Headlines are sentence case — no all-caps headings, and no single word coloured differently inside a heading for emphasis. On dark sections, drop body copy to #C6D3EC rather than pure white; full white is reserved for headings.

05UI elements

Buttons

Start a project View services Learn more

One orange button per screen — it marks the single most valuable action. Blue is the everyday button; bordered white is the quiet third option. Radius 8px, padding 12/22, weight 600. Hover darkens the fill one step (#D93A16, #163D8A); focus draws a 2px #265DC3 ring offset 3px.

Badges and tags

Web application Agentic AI Ecommerce Consulting

Each service line owns one hue, and keeps it across the site, decks and proposals. Tint at roughly 12% over white for the fill; take the text from the same hue darkened to clear 4.5:1.

Surfaces

Cards sit on #FFFFFF with a 1px #E2E6ED border and a 14px radius — no drop shadows. Section bands alternate white and #F3F3F3. Dark bands use the signature gradient or flat #051C50; two gradient bands never touch.

06Voice

Xylozen sells engineering judgement to clients outside India who cannot see the team in person, so the writing carries the credibility the office would. Plain verbs, specific nouns, no adjectives doing the work of evidence. Say what was built, for whom, and what it changed.

The tagline is set as Technology . Innovation . Solutions — spaced periods, sentence case, always on its own line beneath the logo, never as a headline and never inside a sentence. It appears in silver on dark and in #6B7480 on light.

Write"Cut checkout drop-off by 22% for a UK retailer."
Not"We leverage cutting-edge solutions to empower your business."

The text-metal utility reproduces the logo's brushed finish for large display type on dark sections only. Below 40px it reads as flat grey — use --color-silver-100 instead.
